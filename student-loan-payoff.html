How this estimate works

Each month, interest is estimated as balance multiplied by APR divided by 12, then the fixed payment is applied. The final payment is reduced to the remaining amount owed.

This is not an official federal student-loan repayment estimator. It excludes income-driven plans, forgiveness, subsidies, capitalization, daily interest, fees, rate changes and deferment/forbearance. Federal and private loan rules differ.

Understand the repayment estimate

Interest and payment allocation

This calculator models one balance with a fixed annual interest rate and a fixed monthly payment. It estimates one month's interest by multiplying the balance by the annual rate divided by 12, then applies the payment. The final payment is limited to the amount still owed. Real servicers may calculate interest daily, apply payments in a different order or manage multiple loans separately.

Federal and private student loans are different

Federal loans may offer repayment plans, deferment, forbearance, discharge or forgiveness options subject to current program rules and eligibility. Private student loans are governed by their contracts and lender policies and may not offer the same federal protections. A fixed-payment payoff estimate cannot determine which repayment plan is best for a borrower.

Check your account before making changes

Review each loan's current principal, interest rate, accrued interest, repayment status and required payment with the loan servicer. If you have multiple loans, different rates or separate repayment plans, calculate them individually or use an official tool that supports your loan types. Do not stop or change required payments based only on this estimate.

Student loan payoff questions

Does this calculator include federal loan forgiveness?

No. It estimates payoff from a fixed monthly payment and does not model income-driven repayment, Public Service Loan Forgiveness, discharge or other program rules. Check current eligibility and account-specific information at Federal Student Aid.

Why might my servicer's payoff date be different?

Servicers may use daily interest, payment due dates, accrued interest, fees, multiple loan groups, rate changes or other account details that this simplified monthly model does not include.

Can I use this for private student loans?

You can use it for a rough single-loan fixed-rate scenario, but private loan terms vary. Review the promissory note and contact the lender about payment allocation, variable rates, fees and prepayment rules.

See the USAJIO student loan guide for a summary of repayment considerations. For account-specific federal loan information and official repayment tools, visit Federal Student Aid.
