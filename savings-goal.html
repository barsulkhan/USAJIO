How this estimate works

The monthly amount is the remaining target (target minus current savings, never below zero) divided by the number of months available.

Assumes no interest, investment growth, taxes or fees. If current savings already meet the target, the required monthly amount is zero.

How much should you save each month?

The calculator subtracts the amount you have already saved from your target, then divides the remaining amount by the number of months available. For example, a $5,000 goal with $500 already saved and 18 months remaining leaves $4,500 to build. Dividing $4,500 by 18 gives a target contribution of $250 per month.

Make a savings target easier to follow

  • Choose a target amount and a date that fit the purpose of the goal.
  • Keep the money for a near-term goal in an account appropriate for its timing and risk.
  • Schedule a recurring transfer after payday if that suits your cash flow.
  • Review the balance monthly and update the contribution if the target or deadline changes.

Does this savings goal calculator include interest?

No. It assumes contributions earn no interest and excludes investment returns, taxes, fees and changes in prices. If you want to model compound growth on a balance with regular contributions, use the compound interest calculator. For a cash reserve measured in months of expenses, see the emergency fund calculator.
