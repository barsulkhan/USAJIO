How this estimate works

The monthly amount is the remaining target (target minus current savings, never below zero) divided by the number of months available.

Assumes no interest, investment growth, taxes or fees. If current savings already meet the target, the required monthly amount is zero.

Build a savings plan you can maintain

Choose a specific target

Give the goal a clear amount and purpose, such as a car repair reserve, moving costs, a planned purchase or a future trip. If the expense is uncertain, use a reasonable estimate and revisit it as you learn more. Separating near-term savings from everyday spending can make progress easier to track.

Set a realistic deadline

The timeline is a key part of the calculation. A shorter deadline requires a larger monthly contribution; extending the timeline lowers the amount needed each month but delays the goal. Try more than one timeline and compare the result with your take-home pay and regular bills.

Account for money already saved

Enter the amount already set aside for this goal, not money committed to rent, bills or another purpose. The calculator subtracts current savings from the target before dividing the remaining amount by the number of months. If the savings already meet the target, the estimated monthly contribution is zero.

Automate and review contributions

A recurring transfer shortly after payday can help make saving consistent. Review the target after major changes in prices, income or plans. If a contribution is too difficult to sustain, adjust the timeline rather than relying on an unrealistic budget.

Understand the estimate's limits

This calculator assumes no interest, investment return, taxes or account fees. A savings account may earn interest, but rates can change and returns are not guaranteed. For money needed soon, consider access, account terms and deposit protections before choosing where to keep it.

Savings goal calculator questions

How much should I save each month?

For this simple estimate, subtract the amount already saved from your target and divide the remainder by the number of months available. The result is a planning starting point, not a requirement; choose a contribution that fits your budget.

Does the calculator include savings interest?

No. It divides the remaining target evenly across the months and assumes no interest, fees or investment growth. Actual account earnings may change the amount needed.

What if I have already reached my savings goal?

If current savings equal or exceed the target, the estimated monthly contribution is zero. Make sure the amount entered is genuinely available for this goal and is not needed for other expenses.
