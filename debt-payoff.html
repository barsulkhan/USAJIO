How the debt payoff calculator works

The single-debt estimate applies monthly interest to the opening balance, then subtracts your payment. The multi-debt comparison keeps the minimum payment on each active debt and directs the extra amount to one priority balance at a time.

Debt snowball method

The snowball method pays the smallest balance first, while keeping minimum payments on the other debts. When one balance is cleared, its available payment rolls into the next balance. Smaller early wins may help some people stay motivated.

Debt avalanche method

The avalanche method directs extra payments to the debt with the highest APR first. With the same total monthly budget and assumptions, this method often reduces total interest, although the exact result depends on balances, rates and minimum payments.

These are educational estimates, not a recommendation or a lender payoff quote. Actual interest may use daily balances, payment dates, changing rates, fees or different minimum-payment rules. Keep required minimums current and check your lender statements.
