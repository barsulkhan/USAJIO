How to Compare Auto Financing

The monthly payment is only one part of auto financing. Compare the amount financed, APR, loan term, total interest and the full vehicle cost before signing.

Start with the out-the-door price

Separate the vehicle price from taxes, title, registration, dealer documentation charges, add-ons and optional products. Subtract any down payment or trade-in equity to understand the amount you may finance.

Compare APR and loan term

APR helps compare the cost of credit, while the term determines how long payments continue. A longer term can lower the scheduled monthly payment but may increase total interest and the risk of owing more than the vehicle is worth.

Review the complete contract

Check the amount financed, finance charge, total of payments, payment schedule, late fees, optional add-ons and any prepayment conditions. Do not rely only on a dealer's monthly-payment figure.

Consider ownership costs

Insurance, fuel or charging, maintenance, repairs, parking and registration add to the cost of owning a vehicle. Keep room in the budget for these expenses.

Get competing offers

Compare financing from banks, credit unions and other lenders before committing. Ask whether a quoted rate is conditional on credit, vehicle age, down payment or other requirements.
