How this estimate works

Each month, interest is estimated as remaining balance multiplied by APR divided by 12. The fixed payment is then applied; the final payment is reduced to the amount owed.

Assumes no new purchases, fees, rate changes, promotional periods or missed payments. Actual card interest may accrue daily. A payment at or below monthly interest will not reduce principal.
