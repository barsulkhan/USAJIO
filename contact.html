Feedback, corrections and technical issues

Clear feedback helps us find and correct problems in calculators, explanations and site navigation.

Report a problem

USAJIO does not currently publish a dedicated support email address or contact form. For a public, non-sensitive issue, you can report a reproducible bug or suggest a correction through the USAJIO project issue tracker on GitHub.

When reporting an issue, include the page URL, the steps that reproduce the problem, the expected behavior and what happened instead. For a calculation concern, provide sample inputs only when they are fictional and contain no personal information.

Protect your information

Never post passwords, account or routing numbers, payment-card details, government identification numbers, private documents or real household financial figures in a public issue. USAJIO cannot provide account support, review a private financial situation or resolve disputes with banks, lenders or credit bureaus.

Corrections and review

For a factual correction, identify the statement in question and, where possible, include a link to an authoritative source such as a government agency or regulator. Reports are requests for review; submitting one does not guarantee a response time or a particular outcome.
