Privacy Policy

Last reviewed: 21 November 2026

Calculator information

The calculators on this static website perform calculations in your browser. The site code does not send the financial figures you enter to a USAJIO application server. Avoid entering passwords, full account numbers, payment-card details or other sensitive credentials.

Website hosting and technical data

The website is hosted through GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information such as IP address, browser details, request timestamps and requested pages as part of operating and securing its service. Review GitHub's current privacy documentation for details about its processing and retention.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

USAJIO currently has no first-party analytics, advertising or account-registration feature intentionally built into this static site. Third-party services loaded by a browser, including web fonts, may process connection information under their own policies. If analytics, advertising, forms or other third-party tools are added, this policy must be updated before they are enabled.

External links

Links to other websites are provided for convenience. Their privacy practices are controlled by those providers, not USAJIO.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated when site functionality or applicable requirements change. The review date above should be checked after material updates. This page is informational and is not legal advice.
