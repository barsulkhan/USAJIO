Privacy Policy

Last reviewed: 22 November 2026

Calculator information

The calculators on this static website perform calculations in your browser. The site code does not send the financial figures you enter to a USAJIO application server. Avoid entering passwords, full account numbers, payment-card details or other sensitive credentials.

Website hosting and technical data

The website is hosted through GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information such as IP address, browser details, request timestamps and requested pages as part of operating and securing its service. Review GitHub's current privacy documentation for details about its processing and retention.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

USAJIO does not currently intentionally run first-party analytics, advertising or account registration on this static site. Third-party services loaded by a browser, including web fonts, may process connection information under their own policies.

If Google AdSense or another advertising service is enabled in the future, that service and its advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar identifiers to serve, measure and personalise advertisements, subject to the choices available in the relevant product and applicable consent requirements. Third parties may place and read cookies in a visitor's browser, or use web beacons or IP addresses, as a result of ad serving. Advertising will not be enabled until the relevant service details, data practices, choices and required consent mechanism have been reviewed and this policy has been updated to accurately describe the live setup.

For information about Google's use of data when you use partner sites or apps, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. If advertising is enabled, visitors should also review the ad privacy and personalisation controls provided by Google and their browser.

External links

Links to other websites are provided for convenience. Their privacy practices are controlled by those providers, not USAJIO.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated when site functionality or applicable requirements change. The review date above should be checked after material updates. This page is informational and is not legal advice.
