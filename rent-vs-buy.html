Model assumptions: fixed-rate mortgage with monthly amortization; rent rises annually; home value compounds monthly; annual property tax, insurance, maintenance and PMI are approximated monthly. Buyer net cost includes down payment, purchase closing costs, mortgage payments, owner costs and selling costs, less estimated sale proceeds after paying the remaining loan. Renter cost includes rent and renter insurance. A refundable security deposit is excluded.

What this comparison does not include

Taxes or tax deductions, capital-gains tax, inflation-adjusted dollars, investment returns on a renter’s down payment or monthly savings, utilities, moving costs, lender fees, rate changes, local transfer taxes or repair costs above the maintenance allowance. PMI is only an editable estimate and is not automatically removed. Actual costs and loan terms vary by location and lender.

Small changes in assumptions can change the result. Review your local costs and loan documents before making a housing decision.
