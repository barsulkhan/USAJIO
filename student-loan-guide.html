Student Loans: Understand Repayment Choices

Understand student loan interest, repayment options and why official borrower resources matter before changing a loan.

1. Identify each loan

Record lender or servicer, balance, interest rate, whether the rate is fixed or variable, required payment and key dates. Keep government-backed loans separate from private loans because protections and options can differ.

2. Understand interest

Check whether interest accrues daily or monthly, whether unpaid interest can capitalize, and whether fees or rate changes apply. APR divided by 12 is only a simplified monthly approximation and may not match your statement.

3. Compare official repayment options

Eligible US federal borrowers should review current options directly with Federal Student Aid and their loan servicer. Compare payment, total expected cost, eligibility and recertification duties. Private lenders have different contract terms.

4. Look beyond the monthly amount

A lower payment can extend repayment and increase total interest. Before refinancing or consolidating, check whether borrower protections, subsidies, flexible repayment or program eligibility could be lost.

5. Seek help early

Contact the servicer or official student-aid authority promptly if payments become difficult. Be cautious of third parties promising guaranteed forgiveness or special access.

Official information

US borrowers can start with Federal Student Aid at StudentAid.gov and the official servicer named in their account. Private borrowers should review their signed agreement and current account disclosures. Outside the US, consult the relevant government student-finance authority.
