ABOUT USAJIO

Make Smarter Money Decisions

USAJIO is a free educational website offering practical personal-finance calculators and plain-language guides. Its tools cover budgeting, saving, borrowing, debt repayment, credit and long-term planning.

Last reviewed: November 22, 2026

Who the site is for

USAJIO is designed for people who want to explore common money questions, understand the assumptions behind an estimate and compare possible scenarios before making their own decisions. The calculators are general-purpose educational tools, not a substitute for reviewing your actual statements, contracts or personal circumstances.

How our calculators work

  • Each calculator asks for a defined set of inputs and displays an estimate based on the method described on its page.
  • Where relevant, pages explain assumptions such as payment timing, compounding, fixed rates or excluded costs.
  • Automated browser tests check known-answer examples, zero-rate cases, boundaries, invalid inputs and selected repayment edge cases.
  • Currency selectors change how amounts are displayed; they do not perform foreign-exchange conversion.
  • Results can differ from lenders, banks, tax authorities or investment providers because their terms, timing, fees and calculation methods may differ.

How we approach financial information

  • Prefer official institutions and primary sources for laws, rates, benefits, eligibility and consumer protections.
  • Identify the relevant country or jurisdiction instead of implying that one country's rules apply everywhere.
  • Explain important limitations and avoid presenting estimates, projections or examples as guarantees.
  • Review educational pages and update references when material information changes.
  • Keep advertising or commercial relationships, if introduced in the future, separate from editorial judgments.

Privacy and your information

The current site is a static website. Calculator inputs are processed in your browser and are not intended to be submitted to USAJIO. The Net Worth Calculator is designed to keep entered figures in the browser rather than transmit or persist them. Basic hosting or browser requests may still generate technical information outside the calculator itself; read the Privacy Policy for the site's stated practices.

What USAJIO is not

USAJIO is not a bank, lender, broker, credit bureau, tax preparer or government agency. It does not provide individualized financial, investment, tax or legal advice, promise loan approval, guarantee investment returns or determine eligibility for a financial product. You are responsible for checking current terms and seeking qualified help when needed.

Corrections and feedback

If you find a calculation issue, broken link or unclear explanation, use the public project feedback route on our Contact page. Please do not share private financial or account information.
