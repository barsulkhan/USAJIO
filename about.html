ABOUT USAJIO

Make Smarter Money Decisions

USAJIO is a free educational website built around practical personal-finance calculators and plain-language money guides. Its tools cover budgeting, savings, borrowing, debt repayment and long-term planning.

How we approach financial information

  • Explain what each calculator estimates and which assumptions it uses.
  • Make important limitations visible rather than presenting estimates as guarantees.
  • Separate country-specific rules instead of implying that one country's rules apply everywhere.
  • Prefer official institutions and primary sources when discussing laws, rates, benefits or eligibility.
  • Keep calculator inputs in the browser in the current static implementation.

What USAJIO is not

USAJIO is not a financial institution and does not provide individualized financial, investment, tax or legal advice. It does not promise lender approval, investment returns or savings outcomes.
