How this estimate works

Uses a monthly equivalent rate derived from the assumed annual return. The current balance compounds monthly; each monthly contribution (plus the user-entered match percentage) is assumed to be deposited at month-end.

The optional scenario section adjusts projected balances into today's dollars using a constant inflation assumption. Fees, taxes, contribution limits, vesting, market volatility, contribution increases and withdrawals are not modeled. Returns and inflation are assumptions, not guarantees; actual investments can lose value.
