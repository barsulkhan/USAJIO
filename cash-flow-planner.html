How this forecast works

The planner starts with the balance on your selected start date. It adds income and subtracts expenses on their expected dates, then carries each day's ending balance forward. Weekly totals show money in, money out and the projected balance at the end of each seven-day period.

Recurring items can be weekly, every two weeks or monthly. One-time items occur once. Events on the same date are combined before that day's balance is calculated. A negative projected balance flags a possible shortfall; it does not account for overdraft protection, pending transactions, payment processing times or unlisted spending.

This is a user-entered estimate, not a real-time account forecast. Variable income, irregular spending, transfers between your own accounts and changes to due dates can affect actual balances. Review the plan regularly. USAJIO provides educational tools, not individualized financial advice.
