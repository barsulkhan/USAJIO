Mortgage Basics: Principal, Interest and Home Costs

A mortgage is a long-term loan secured by a home. The monthly principal-and-interest payment is only one part of the cost of owning a property.

Principal and interest

Principal is the amount borrowed. Interest is the lender's charge for lending the money. A fixed-rate mortgage generally has a scheduled principal-and-interest payment that stays level, while the share going to principal typically rises over time.

Down payment and loan amount

The down payment is the portion of the purchase price paid upfront. A larger down payment usually means a smaller loan, but buyers should also preserve cash for closing costs, moving, repairs and reserves. Some loans may require private mortgage insurance (PMI) or other mortgage insurance depending on the program and down payment.

Other monthly housing costs

  • Property taxes, which vary by location and may change.
  • Homeowners insurance and, where relevant, flood or other coverage.
  • Homeowners association (HOA) dues, if applicable.
  • Maintenance, utilities and repairs.

Some lenders collect taxes and insurance through an escrow account; others may handle them differently. A calculator estimate is not an escrow statement or lender quote.
