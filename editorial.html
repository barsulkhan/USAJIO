OUR STANDARDS

Editorial Policy

Last reviewed: 22 November 2026

USAJIO publishes general personal-finance education and browser-based calculators. Our goal is to explain common money concepts in plain language, show the assumptions behind estimates and help readers identify questions they may need to check with an official source or qualified professional.

Research and source selection

  • For laws, taxes, government benefits, lending rules, consumer rights and official program eligibility, prefer the responsible government agency, regulator or program administrator.
  • For financial education, prefer established public-interest institutions and clearly identify the country or jurisdiction the information applies to.
  • Distinguish facts from examples, estimates, opinions and assumptions. Do not present an illustrative example as a current rate, offer or personal recommendation.
  • Link readers to the primary source so they can confirm current details. Third-party summaries may provide context but should not replace an available authoritative source.

Calculator standards

  • Explain the inputs, calculation method and important assumptions that materially affect a result.
  • Identify material exclusions such as fees, taxes, changing rates, contract terms, timing differences or eligibility rules where relevant.
  • Use test cases for ordinary values, boundaries, zero-rate cases and invalid inputs. Correct material calculation errors when identified.
  • Describe outputs as estimates, not lender quotes, account statements, tax determinations, investment forecasts or guarantees.

Jurisdiction and review dates

Financial rules vary by country and can change. Pages focused on a particular country should identify that scope clearly. Where information can become outdated, show a review date and revisit the relevant official sources when the page is materially updated. A review date does not mean every external rule is continuously monitored.

Corrections and feedback

We aim to correct confirmed factual, calculation, broken-link and clarity issues. When a material correction is made, the affected page should be rechecked and its review date updated. Readers can use the contact information published on the Contact page; do not send passwords, full account numbers or other sensitive financial information.

Editorial independence and advertising

Advertising, sponsorship or affiliate relationships must not determine calculator results, source selection or editorial conclusions. Paid relationships, if introduced, should be clearly identified. Advertisements must be distinguishable from editorial content and must not be positioned in a way that encourages accidental clicks or interferes with calculator controls, navigation or results. USAJIO currently does not intentionally serve advertisements.

Scope and limitations

USAJIO is an educational resource, not a bank, lender, broker, tax preparer, law firm or personal financial adviser. Content is general information and may not reflect every reader's circumstances. Readers should verify important decisions using current official documents and appropriately qualified advice.
