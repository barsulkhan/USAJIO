How net worth is calculated

Net worth = total assets − total liabilities. Assets can include cash, investments, retirement accounts and the current estimated value of property or vehicles. Liabilities include outstanding loan, credit-card and other debt balances.

Use a consistent valuation date and reasonable current estimates. This snapshot excludes future income, taxes due, selling costs, unvested benefits and assets or debts you do not enter. Currency selection changes display formatting only; it does not convert exchange rates. This tool is educational and is not a valuation, lending or tax assessment.
