How this estimate works

Total expenses are the sum of housing, food, transport, bills and other expenses. Money remaining equals monthly take-home income minus those expenses and any savings/debt contributions; the remaining share is that amount divided by income.

This is a monthly planning snapshot, not a forecast. Enter take-home income and all amounts for the same monthly period; a negative remainder means planned outflows exceed income. The 50/30/20 allocation is optional and editable.
