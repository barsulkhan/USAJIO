Calculator estimates and assumptions

Calculator outputs depend on the values you enter and the assumptions described on each tool. Results may use simplified monthly interest, fixed-rate, regular-payment or no-growth assumptions. They may exclude fees, taxes, insurance, escrow, daily interest, promotional rates, contract terms, eligibility rules, market changes or other factors. Results are estimates—not lender quotes, payoff statements, account records, tax calculations, investment forecasts or guarantees.

Check that all inputs use the same currency and time period. Compare results with current statements, written offers and official documents. If an estimate differs from a lender or account statement, the official statement and contract terms control.
