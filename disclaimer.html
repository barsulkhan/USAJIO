Financial Disclaimer

Last reviewed: 24 November 2026

USAJIO provides general educational information, budgeting resources and simplified financial calculation tools. The website is intended to help visitors explore hypothetical scenarios and understand common personal-finance concepts. It does not provide individualized financial, investment, tax, accounting, credit, mortgage or legal advice, and it does not assess a visitor's complete financial circumstances, goals, eligibility or risk tolerance.

Calculator estimates and assumptions

Calculator results are estimates based on the information entered by the visitor and the methods described on each calculator page. They may use simplified assumptions about interest, payment timing, compounding, fees, taxes, inflation, rates or account balances. Results are not bank statements, lender quotes, payoff letters, loan approvals, tax calculations, investment forecasts or guarantees. Rounding, lender servicing practices, contract terms and changes in rates or costs can produce different real-world results.

Some tools intentionally exclude costs or features that are difficult to estimate consistently, such as escrow adjustments, insurance, taxes, fees, variable rates, loan forgiveness, penalties, maintenance or investment risk. Each calculator's methodology and exclusions should be read before relying on an estimate. Currency selectors may change display formatting only and do not necessarily convert one currency into another.

Financial decisions and professional advice

Do not make a financial transaction, borrow money, refinance a loan, change a student-loan repayment plan, buy or sell an investment, or miss a required payment solely because of a USAJIO result. Verify important figures with the relevant bank, lender, servicer, government agency or account provider. Consider speaking with an appropriately qualified and independent professional when a decision has significant financial, tax or legal consequences.

Investment values can fall as well as rise, and past performance does not guarantee future results. Debt, mortgage and vehicle-financing costs may include charges that are not represented in a calculator. Government programs, tax rules, consumer protections, rates and eligibility requirements can change; consult current official information for your circumstances.

Third-party information and external links

USAJIO may link to government agencies, regulators, educational organizations and other third-party websites for further reading. These links are provided for convenience and context. USAJIO does not control every external page, guarantee that its content remains current, or endorse every product, service or statement on a linked website. Confirm current terms and details directly with the relevant organization.

Advertising and editorial independence

Where advertising or other commercial content appears, it should not be treated as a recommendation or as a reason to choose a financial product. Advertising does not change calculator formulas or the educational purpose of the site's content. Visitors should compare products independently and review all applicable disclosures and terms.

Corrections and questions

If you notice a calculation issue, outdated information or a broken source link, please use the USAJIO contact page to report it. For information about how content is researched and reviewed, read the Editorial Policy.

This disclaimer applies to the USAJIO website and its educational tools unless a page states a more specific limitation. Continued use of a calculator does not create a professional advisory relationship.
