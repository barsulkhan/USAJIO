The schedule shows the first 12 months only. The payoff estimate uses the same monthly rule through the final payment.

Model: monthly interest = balance × APR ÷ 12. Minimum payment = the greater of (balance plus interest) × the entered percentage or the entered floor, capped at the amount owed. No new purchases, fees, rate changes or promotional balances are included. Many issuers calculate interest daily and use different minimum-payment rules; check your statement or card agreement for exact terms.

How this estimate works

Interest is added once per month using APR divided by 12. The minimum is then recalculated from the interest-inclusive balance using your entered percentage and floor. The payment is capped at the amount due, and the remaining balance carries into the next month.

This simplified model does not reproduce an issuer's exact billing system, average-daily-balance method, fees, penalty APR, multiple APR buckets, grace period or payment allocation. If the selected rule cannot reduce the balance, no payoff date is shown.
