How this estimate works

Principal is home price less the down-payment percentage. Principal and interest use fixed-rate monthly amortization. The monthly total adds annual property tax and homeowners insurance divided by 12, plus HOA.

Not an escrow quote. Excludes PMI, utilities, maintenance, closing costs and possible tax/insurance changes.

What is included in a monthly mortgage payment?

Principal is the amount borrowed after the down payment. Interest is the cost of borrowing that principal. This calculator estimates principal and interest using a fixed rate and regular monthly payments. It then adds the annual property-tax estimate and homeowners-insurance estimate divided by 12, plus the monthly HOA amount you enter.

Costs to check separately

The result is not a lender's escrow quote or a complete cost of homeownership. Private mortgage insurance (PMI), mortgage insurance premiums, utilities, maintenance, repairs, closing costs, discount points and changes in taxes or insurance are excluded. Your lender may collect some costs through escrow, but escrow amounts can change over time.

Compare loan offers using the same assumptions

When comparing lenders, use the same home price, down payment, loan type and term. Review the APR, lender fees, points, cash due at closing and total interest—not just the advertised rate or monthly payment. A shorter term can mean a higher monthly payment but less total interest. A longer term can reduce the payment while increasing the total interest paid.
