How this estimate works

Principal is home price less the down-payment percentage. Principal and interest use fixed-rate monthly amortization. The monthly total adds annual property tax and homeowners insurance divided by 12, plus HOA.

Not an escrow quote. Excludes PMI, utilities, maintenance, closing costs and possible tax/insurance changes.

How mortgage inputs affect the estimate

Down payment and loan amount

The down payment is the portion of the purchase price you pay upfront. The remaining amount is the starting principal used in this estimate. A larger down payment reduces the loan balance and may affect whether private mortgage insurance is required; this calculator does not estimate PMI.

Interest rate and loan term

The interest rate and repayment term both affect principal-and-interest payments. A longer term generally spreads repayment over more months and may reduce the monthly payment, but it can increase the total interest paid over the life of the loan. Compare the total cost as well as the payment that fits your monthly budget.

Taxes, insurance and HOA

Property tax and homeowners insurance are entered as annual amounts and divided by 12 for a monthly estimate. HOA dues are added as a monthly amount. These costs can change over time, and actual escrow deposits may differ from a simple monthly average.

Costs not included

This estimate does not include private mortgage insurance, mortgage insurance premiums, utilities, maintenance, repairs, closing costs, discount points or changes in taxes and insurance. It is not a loan offer or an escrow analysis. Ask lenders for a written Loan Estimate to compare actual offers and fees.

Mortgage calculator questions

Does the monthly total include property tax and insurance?

Yes, if you enter those annual amounts. The calculator divides each by 12 and adds them to principal and interest, along with the monthly HOA amount. Actual bills and escrow payments can vary.

Does this estimate include PMI?

No. Private mortgage insurance depends on loan type, down payment, lender requirements and other factors. Obtain an estimate from the lender and add it separately to your housing budget.

Is a 30-year mortgage always cheaper per month?

It often has a lower scheduled payment than a shorter term at the same rate, but interest may accrue over a longer period. Compare total interest, fees and your plans for the property.
