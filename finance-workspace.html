Privacy & data

Your workspace, your device, your choice.

Encrypted local vault

All workspace records are encrypted with AES-GCM before being saved in this browser's IndexedDB. Your passphrase is never saved or sent to a server. If you clear browser data or lose your passphrase, use your encrypted backup to restore access.

Encryption helps protect data at rest on this device. It does not protect an unlocked session from malicious extensions, compromised devices, or scripts running on this site. Never enter bank passwords, card numbers, or account credentials here.
