How this estimate works

Individual card utilization = that card's balance ÷ its credit limit × 100. Overall utilization = the sum of entered balances ÷ the sum of entered credit limits × 100. Target paydown = the amount by which total balances exceed the selected target percentage of total limits.

The estimate assumes credit limits stay unchanged and that payments reduce reported balances dollar for dollar. Issuers and scoring models may use balances reported at different times. Avoid carrying interest-bearing debt solely to influence a credit score.
