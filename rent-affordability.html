How this estimate works

Cash available for rent and utilities = take-home income − debt payments − other essential expenses − planned savings. The guideline amount = gross monthly income × selected percentage. Suggested rent is the lower of the guideline amount and cash available after estimated utilities.

This is a budgeting estimate, not a guarantee of affordability, lease approval or a recommendation to spend the full amount. Include renters insurance, parking, deposits, moving expenses and irregular bills when planning.
