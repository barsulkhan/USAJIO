How this estimate works

Debt-to-income ratio = recurring monthly debt payments ÷ gross monthly income × 100. This page uses the amounts you enter. It does not include groceries, utilities, insurance, subscriptions or other non-debt living expenses unless you choose to include them as other debt.

Lender standards and debt definitions vary by loan type, program and borrower circumstances. Do not treat a percentage band as a guarantee of approval or affordability.
